My Favorite Recipe

Now I will share my favorite recipe with you. This is a classic Filipino dish called "Filipino Adobo".

Ingredients

  • 1 kg chicken or pork, cut into pieces
  • 1/2 cup soy sauce
  • 1/2 cup vinegar
  • 1 head garlic, minced
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2-3 bay leaves
  • 1 tsp black peppercorns
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Heat the cooking oil in a large pan over medium heat.
  2. Sauté the garlic and onion until fragrant.
  3. Add the chicken or pork and cook until browned.
  4. Pour in the soy sauce and vinegar. Do not stir immediately.
  5. Add the bay leaves, black peppercorns, and water.
  6. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 30-40 minutes until the meat is tender.
  7. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  8. Serve hot with steamed rice.

Notes

Filipino Adobo is the national dish of the Philippines and is different from region to region and family to family.
This is my family's version, which I find to be the most flavorful and comforting.

Tip: Marinate the meat in the soy sauce and vinegar mixture for at least 30 minutes before cooking to enhance the flavor.
